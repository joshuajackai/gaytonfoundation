Contact

Contact the foundation

We read everything that comes in. Whether you want to sponsor the event, ask about a gift, request our IRS determination letter, or send a note to the Gayton family, this is the place to start.

What can we help with

Sponsorships

Corporate and individual sponsorship of Boots on the Ground, our December 5, 2026 fundraiser. We will send the sponsorship packet and talk through levels.

Tickets and donations

Questions about tickets, tables, gifts, checks by mail, or a donation receipt for your records.

Grants and partnerships

If your organization works in community, housing, early childhood education, or social justice in the Puget Sound region, tell us about it.

Media and verification

Press enquiries, and requests for our IRS determination letter or state registration from funders and platforms. We send those documents directly on request.
