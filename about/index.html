About the Foundation

Chandler Walker Gayton Foundation

The Chandler Walker Gayton Foundation is a Washington nonprofit corporation with 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status, formed to honor the life of Chandler Walker Gayton by continuing the causes he cared about most.

Official website and domain

gaytonfoundation.org is the official website and official domain of the Chandler Walker Gayton Foundation. It is owned and operated by the foundation, and it is our only website.

The domain is a shortened form of our registered legal name. Email addresses ending in @gaytonfoundation.org belong to the foundation and are used by our officers and staff for official correspondence.

What the foundation supports

Chandler gave his time and attention to the people around him. The foundation carries that forward through four areas of work.

  • Community, keeping neighbors connected and supported
  • Housing, helping families find and hold stable homes
  • Early childhood education, giving children a steady start
  • Elder care, making sure older neighbors are not left alone
