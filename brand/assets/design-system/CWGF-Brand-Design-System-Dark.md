## 3. Rules that never bend

1. **Copy facts exactly.** Names, titles, dates, addresses, the EIN, and mission language come word for word from this kit.
2. **No em dashes or en dashes.** Use a comma, a period, or a center dot (·). Write date ranges as "1990 to 2024".
3. **No pure black or pure white.** Use warm near-black #0E0905 and warm white #F5EDE0. The one exception is the on-dark wordmark file, which is white #FFFFFF.
4. **Headlines are never bold.** Playfair Display at 400. Names and card titles may use 500. Nothing heavier.
5. **Chandler lived 33 years.** Chandler Walker Gayton, 1990 to 2024.
6. **Four focus areas, in this order.** Community, Housing, Early Childhood Education, Social Justice.
7. **Warm, plain, dignified.** No hype, no slogans, no exclamation points. Name the work and the people, not the grief.
8. **Keep private documents private.** Say "501(c)(3) approved" and give the EIN. Never publish the IRS determination letter; send it one to one on request.
9. **Husky Purple or darker, never pink.** The only purple is UW Husky Purple #32006E and its darker shades (#23004D, #15002E), always solid, never see-through, and never faded into orange, copper, or cream. No violet, lavender, lilac, Spirit Purple, pink, rose, blush, mauve, magenta, or wine in any tint, gradient stop, overlay, or photo filter. Accents on purple are champagne #BE9A71 or warm white.

## 4. Plain-word glossary

- **Token**: a named value, like a color or a size, that designers and code share so they stay in sync. Example: `--champagne` is #BE9A71.
- **Weight**: how thick a typeface is drawn. 300 Light, 400 Regular, 500 Medium, 700 Bold.
- **Eyebrow**: the small uppercase label that sits above a headline.
- **Em**: the italic phrase inside a headline that carries the accent color, like "believed in." in "Fund the work Chandler believed in."
- **Hairline**: a 1 pixel line used for borders and dividers.
- **Glass**: a see-through card fill (champagne at 7%) over the dark background.
- **Contrast ratio**: how readable text is on its background. Body text needs 4.5:1 or more; large text (24 px and up) needs 3:1. This is the WCAG AA standard.

## 6. Mission, vision and values

Official language, updated September 2026 from the Bylaws (Article 2.1) and the Articles of Incorporation (Article 3.1). Use it verbatim.

**Mission (full statement)**

The Chandler Walker Gayton Foundation carries forward Chandler Walker Gayton's love for his community by funding the causes he cared about most: community, housing stability, early childhood education, and social justice. We do this through grants to aligned 501(c)(3) charities, scholarships and stipends for disadvantaged students pursuing education and vocational training, support for public schools, and administrative support to other tax-exempt organizations doing similar work.

**Mission (short statement)**

We fund the causes Chandler Walker Gayton cared about most: community, housing stability, early childhood education, and social justice, in his memory and in his name.

**Website mission headline**: Fund the work Chandler believed in.

**Tagline**: Honoring a life. Extending his causes.

**Vision**

A community where every neighborhood offers a stable home, every child a strong start, and every person the fairness and opportunity Chandler believed they deserved, his generosity carried forward by the people who loved him.

**Core values**

- **Community.** Chandler showed up for people. We invest close to home, in the neighborhoods and relationships that mattered to him.
- **Dignity.** A stable home, a strong start in childhood, and a fair chance at opportunity are not privileges. We believe they are what every person deserves.
- **Generosity.** We give the way Chandler lived: openly and personally, expecting nothing in return.
- **Stewardship.** As a public charity, we hold every dollar entrusted to us with the transparency and accountability the people who gave it deserve.
- **Legacy.** We turn grief into lasting good, keeping Chandler's causes alive well beyond a single night, gift, or year.

## 7. Voice

Traits: Warm · Celebratory · Dignified · Storytelling · Plainspoken

Principle: Celebrate the life, not the loss. Short, vivid sentences. Invite people in with boots, brims, moonshine, and stories, and stay dignified: this is a memorial fundraiser.

Do:
- Use Chandler's full name on first mention, then "Chandler".
- Lead with the people and the work: "We fund the work Chandler believed in."
- Join short facts with a center dot: "Saturday, December 5, 2026 · D-Squared".

Avoid:
- Em dashes, en dashes, exclamation points, and all-caps sentences.
- Slogans, hype, and borrowed corporate phrases.
- Dwelling on loss or describing how Chandler died.

Voice in action (headlines from the website; the italic part is the em):

- An Evening of *Legacy*
- A life lived *wide open*, and the people he gathered.
- An evening built *the way he would have built it.*
- The voices *holding the room.*
- Come dressed for the occasion. *Come ready to tell a story.*

The four focus areas (website copy, verbatim):

- **Community**: We back the people, gatherings, and traditions that hold a neighborhood together.
- **Housing**: We support housing, homelessness relief, and affordability so more families can find a place to stay.
- **Early Childhood Education**: We fund programs that give young children a strong beginning in the years that shape them most.
- **Social Justice**: We back organizations working for fairness, equity, and opportunity in the communities Chandler served.

## 11. Purple gradients

The remembrance purples, extended into full gradients for backgrounds, bands, and light. The only purple is UW Husky Purple and its darker shades, always solid, so nothing reads pink or lavender.

Purple scale:

| Name | Hex | Token | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Husky night | #15002E | --husky-night | Deepest purple. Band edges, backgrounds, gradient ends |
| Husky deep | #23004D | --husky-deep | Panels and gradient middles |
| Husky Purple | #32006E | --husky-purple | UW Husky Purple, the lightest purple allowed. Badges, pills, band centers, solid type on cream |

Gradients:

- **Night bloom**: `linear-gradient(160deg, #0E0905 0%, #15002E 48%, #32006E 100%)`. Full backgrounds: social posts, covers, screens.
- **Husky velvet**: `linear-gradient(158deg, #15002E 0%, #23004D 55%, #32006E 100%)`. Feature panels. Husky Purple is the brightest point.
- **Husky glow**: `radial-gradient(70% 90% at 50% 0%, #32006E 0%, #15002E 72%)`. Light behind a headline or portrait.
- **Remembrance band**: `linear-gradient(90deg, #15002E 0%, #32006E 50%, #15002E 100%)`. Name bands and In Memoriam strips (live on /event).
- **Ember to night**: `linear-gradient(135deg, #6B2508 0%, #2C150D 45%, #0E0905 100%)`. Fundraiser panels. If a piece also needs remembrance, give purple its own band.
- **Husky to night**: `linear-gradient(180deg, #32006E 0%, #15002E 55%, #0E0905 100%)`. Closing panels that settle from purple into near-black.

Type on purple:

- Headlines and body: warm white #F5EDE0 on every purple background (13:1 on Husky Purple).
- Italic em and eyebrow: champagne #BE9A71 (6:1 on Husky Purple). No lavender.
- Buttons stay copper. Purple is atmosphere and remembrance, never the call to action.
- Badges: solid Husky Purple #32006E fill, warm white text, no outline (live on /event). No see-through purple.
- Example post (Husky glow): eyebrow "In Loving Memory" in champagne, headline "An Evening of *Legacy*" in warm white with the em in champagne, date line in champagne, champagne wordmark.

Never:

- Purple lighter than Husky: No violet, lavender, lilac, or Spirit Purple. UW Husky Purple #32006E is the lightest purple allowed.
- Blends and tints: No orange, copper, or cream fading into purple, and no purple at partial opacity. Both turn wine or mauve.
- Pink in any form: No pink, rose, blush, mauve, magenta, or wine, as a color, a tint, a gradient stop, or a photo filter.

## 13. Layout, components and photography

**Spacing** follows a 4 px base: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 80, 96 px (tokens --space-1 to --space-24). Max content width 1240 px; side gutter 20 to 48 px. Section padding about 96 to 128 px top and bottom. Print conversion: 1 px = 0.75 pt.

**Corner radii**: 6 px small tags · 12 px icon tiles, notes, compact cards · 20 px cards · 28 px feature panels · 999 px buttons, pills, and portrait circles.

**Elevation**: card `0 4px 24px` base at 55% · glow `0 0 80px rgba(181,106,37,0.25), 0 0 40px rgba(181,106,37,0.18)` · glass `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(245,237,224,0.06), 0 8px 32px` base · button `0 6px 24px rgba(181,106,37,0.35), inset 0 1px 0 rgba(245,237,224,0.18)`. The website uses black at 40 to 45% for card shadows; for new work use base #0E0905 at the same strength, never pure black.

**Components**

- **Primary button**: pill, Lato 400, 12 to 13 px, 0.18em, uppercase, copper gradient #A35F21 to #84644A, label #FDF9F1, rust glow. Example: "Reserve Your Place".
- **Ghost button**: pill, warm white at 4% fill, champagne hairline at 18%, warm white label. Example: "See Ticket Options".
- **Meta pill**: sits above the hero headline. Midnight at 70% fill, champagne hairline, glowing 6 px rust dot, Lato 300, 11 px, 0.22em, uppercase, champagne. Example: "In Memoriam · 1990 to 2024".
- **Glass feature card**: glass fill, 20 px corners, 40 px icon tile in the rust-to-saddle gradient, Playfair 400 title, Cormorant 300 text.
- **Host card**: 3:4 portrait, 20 px corners, glass badge with the event role (top left), name in Playfair 400 and relationship in Lato 300 uppercase on a shade that fades to base at 85%.
- **Stat**: Playfair 400 number in the stat gradient over a Lato 300 uppercase champagne label. Live stats: 33 Years of Life · 280 Guests Expected · ∞ His Legacy.
- **Pill**: Lato 300, 9.5 px, 0.2em, uppercase, champagne text, forest at 40% fill, champagne hairline. Examples: Open Bar, Live Auction.
- **Choice card**: glass, 20 px corners, Lato 300 uppercase price band in champagne, Playfair 500 title, Lato 300 description. Example: "$150 to $1,000 · Just me · One seat, or two. Come as a guest."
- **Remembrance band**: a slow scrolling line of words in Playfair italic, champagne, separated by rust stars (✦), on the solid Husky Purple band. Use it once per piece.

**Headshot recipe (both editions)**

- Background: vertical gradient, warm charcoal #26231E at the top to bronze #B56A26 at the bottom.
- Subject: cut out cleanly and converted to full black and white. Keep hair and shoulders whole; no halos.
- Framing: shoulders up, face centered, clear space above and to the sides of the head. Match the scale of the existing set rather than cropping tight.
- Files: 800 x 1000 px (4:5) for officers, honorary chairs, and the executive committee; 600 x 600 px for advisors. JPEG, quality 85.
- No photo yet: initials in Playfair Display 500 on midnight #19100A with a champagne hairline, the same size as a photo.
- On the event page: host cards are 3:4 with 20 px corners, a shade that fades to base at 85%, and a glass badge.
- Documentary photos: hero photos stay in warm color, slightly desaturated (85%) and darkened under text with plain near-black, never a colored wash. Tribute and What to Expect photos are black and white.
- Never colorize a black-and-white photo, and never crop people out of group photos.
