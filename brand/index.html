The wordmark

The wordmark is the foundation's name set as a logo. It comes in four colors. Pick the one made for the background you are placing it on.

Use on near-black backgrounds and on the dark ember gradient. This is the version on our website and on event pieces.

Boots on the Ground An Evening of Legacy

The event name

Boots on the Ground has no separate logo file. Set the name in type: Playfair Display, uppercase, in warm white, with An Evening of Legacy beneath it in Lato, uppercase, in champagne. Keep the foundation wordmark nearby as the signature.

Using the wordmark

Clear space
Leave room on every side equal to the height of the letter C in CHANDLER.
Smallest size
1.1 inches wide in print. 140 pixels wide on screen.
For printers
Send the On light or On dark file to printers, sign shops, and embroiderers.
Please do not
Stretch or squash it, recolor it outside the four colors here, add shadows, glows, or outlines, set it on a busy photo, retype the name as a logo, or rotate it.

Color

One brand, two looks. We call each look an edition. Dark surfaces are a warm near-black and light surfaces a warm off-white, never pure black or pure white.

Foundation edition

The light look on gaytonfoundation.org. Use it for anything about the foundation's ongoing work: letters, reports, grant materials.

Event edition

The dark look for Boots on the Ground. Use it for invitations, sponsor materials, event posts, and screens and signs on the night.
